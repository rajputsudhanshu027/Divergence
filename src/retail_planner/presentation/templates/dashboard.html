Explain these results

Errors measure how far estimates missed recorded sales. They are not accuracy percentages. Range coverage counts daily sales observations inside the estimated interval. These figures cover the full sample, not one selected product.

Help and definitions

Forecast

An estimate of future sales. The shaded range shows daily uncertainty, not a guarantee.

Cushion

An adjustment based on past forecast errors. Higher settings produce equal or larger orders.

Baseline

A simple comparison that repeats the previous 28 days of sales.

Historical test

Sales set aside when choosing the method, then used to check its predictions.

Study method and limitations

The stock scenario assumes one immediately delivered order and seven days of starting stock. The data cannot distinguish low demand from unavailable stock. Forecasts support review; real ordering needs actual inventory and delivery information.
